Featured Case Study Ranchi • 4BHK Turnkey Transformation

From Builder Concrete to Warm Editorial Sanctuary

1. The Client Brief: A senior physician family in Morabadi required clutter-free entertaining zones, an ergonomic German modular kitchen for multi-course family meals, and serene acoustic master bedrooms insulated from city noise.

2. The 3D Conception: We generated complete photorealistic renders and 1:20 scale isometric cabinetry drawings, synchronizing false ceiling ambient profiles with bespoke wall fluting.

3. Factory Execution: All carcass units were pre-cut on automated CNC edge-banders using BWR-grade marine ply and soft-close Blum runners, preventing on-site sawdust and ensuring millimeter perfection.
